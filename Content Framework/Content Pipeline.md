# Content Pipeline

## 1. Idea Generation Flow (Trigger-First)
Ideas are generated by looking at what actually happened today.

- **The Trigger:** What happened today? (A bug I couldn't fix, a lecture that confused me, a conversation in the library).
- **Identify the Stage:** Am I learning a new concept (Educational), building a specific feature (Project), or reflecting on a struggle (Personal Opinion)?
- **Check the Message:** Does this show that I am learning, building, or growing through real experience?
- **Apply the Tone:** Keep it as a classmate's perspective. Allow for "I don't know" moments.

## 2. Format Selection Logic
Choose the format based on the required depth:

- **Carousel (Deep Dive):** Use to document a full "Aha!" moment or a technical concept I finally mastered. (Slide 2: The Trigger is mandatory).
- **Reels (Quick Impact):** Use for "Facepalm" hooks and quick technical observations. (Environment mandate applies).
- **Stories (Daily Journey):** Use for daily unpolished updates, "WIP Loops," and low-friction polls.

## 3. Content Transformation Flow

### Reel
- **Hook:** A "Facepalm" hook (e.g., "I almost gave up on this...").
- **Script:** Document the struggle and the specific fix I found. Use "I learned" language.
- **Visuals:** Show the terminal/IDE. Include a clip of the actual study environment.

### Carousel
- **Hook (Slide 1):** Approachable title about a personal realization.
- **The Trigger (Slide 2):** Why I had to learn this specific thing today.
- **Body:** Breakdown of what I learned.
- **Project Bridge:** Show the concept inside the "Car Rental System" code.
- **CTA:** Beginner-friendly community question.

### Stories
- **WIP Loop:** Show the broken state -> Show the fix.
- **Human Side:** Add one "Unpolished" clip (coffee, desk, etc.).
- **Interaction:** Add a binary Poll or Reaction Slider.

## 4. Execution Rules
- **Expert Filter Check:** Before posting, ask: "Does this sound like a teacher or a classmate?" If it sounds like a teacher ("You should..."), rewrite it as a classmate ("I found...").
- **Visual Vibe Check:** Does this look too clean or AI-generated? If yes, add more "visual realism" (messy desk, screen glare, campus clips).
- **Stay Student-Centric:** Integrate university reality (exams, deadlines) into the technical narrative.
- **Avoid Low-Quality Noise:** No generic advice or fake confidence.
