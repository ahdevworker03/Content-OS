# Stories

Stories are for daily presence and building familiarity.
They should feel unpolished, interactive, and grounded in a student's day.

Stories should feel like:

- A real student’s day (including non-coding moments)
- A real learning journey
- A behind-the-scenes view (messy desk included)
- A casual but intentional update

---

## 1) Learning Journey & WIP Loops

### Purpose

Show what I am currently struggling with or learning.

### What to post

- **WIP Loops:** Show the "ugly" UI or the broken terminal screen before the fix.
- What topic I am studying right now.
- The "I don't know yet" moments.
- What finally made sense after a long session.

---

## 2) Unpolished 20% (The Human Side)

### Purpose

Build familiarity through non-technical student life.

### What to post

- Coffee runs / Study snacks
- Desk setup (clean or messy)
- Library sessions
- Exam prep / University stress
- Laptop stickers / Terminal themes

---

## 3) Quick Value

### Purpose

Share a tiny lesson from the day's learning.

---

## 4) Interaction (Friction Reduction)

### Purpose

Create easy engagement with fellow students.

### Rule

Replace open-ended Question Boxes with binary/low-friction stickers:
- **Polls:** "React or Vanilla JS?", "Vim or VS Code?"
- **Reaction Sliders:** "How much do you hate CSS today?"
- **This or That:** Simple tech/study choices.

---

## 5) Project Updates

### Purpose

Share small wins from the Car Rental System.
Only post when the update is real—don't force it.
