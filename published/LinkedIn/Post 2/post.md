When I first entered Computer Science, I thought the field was mainly about programming.

But after spending time learning and exploring, I realized how broad the tech world actually is.

Computer Science is not one path. It is a collection of different fields, each solving problems in its own way.

Some of the major areas include:

• Software Engineering:

Building applications and systems people use every day — from frontend interfaces to backend systems, mobile apps, APIs, and cloud infrastructure.

• Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning:

Creating systems that can learn patterns, make predictions, and improve through data.

• Cybersecurity:

Protecting systems, networks, and data through security analysis, ethical hacking, and system protection.

What I find interesting is that most students do not immediately know which path fits them best.

A lot of us discover our interests by building projects, trying different technologies, making mistakes, and learning step by step.

Right now, I’m exploring software engineering starting with frontend development while continuing to learn more about the field every day.
