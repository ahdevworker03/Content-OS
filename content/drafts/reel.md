## Hook

## Spoken Script

بلّشت سنتي التانية بـ CS، وهي هي الأمور الي رح ركز عليها خلال السنة

أول شي، بدي ركز عالمواد الأساسية مثل databases، software engineering، networking لأنه فيها المفاهيم الأساسية لبناء الsoftwares الحقيقية.

تاني شي، بدي اتعمّق بلغة وحدة استعملها بالـ web development. اللي هي TypeScript لأنه بقدر ابني فيها الfrontend ب React والbackend بexpress. والdatabase من خلال prisma ORM فهي لغة كافية للتعامل مع الsystem كله

بعدها، بدي إفهم كيف الـ systems بتشتغل ، كيف بيحكي الfrontend مع الbackend، كيف بيتحمل الsystem الreal users بدون ما يوقع , كيف نعمل scale للdatabase لتتحمل traffic عالي، وكل هيدا بصير بتجربة بناء حقيقية للsystem مع users حقيقيين

وآخر شي، بدي اعرف كيف اعرض فكرتي ومهاراتي للناس، وكيف وكيف اتواصل مع الأعلم مني بالمجال لأتعلم منه، لأنه المنتج القوي بدون حضور بالسوق ما حرا بيعرفه

## Visual Direction

- Record in a real student setting such as campus, a library, or a desk with university material and a privacy-safe workspace visible.
- Deliver the reflection naturally to camera or while moving through the real study and development environment. Do not use a polished studio voiceover.
- Follow the same progression as the spoken script: core-subject notes, a privacy-safe TypeScript/React/Node context, broad system-building activity, then a simple presentation or explanation context.
- For the systems section, show generic development activity that suggests frontend/backend communication, data movement, testing, traffic, or debugging without exposing a private implementation.
- For the final section, show generic notes or a presentation/documentation context focused on explaining an idea and its value. Do not show private client or product information.
- Crop or blur project names, file paths, credentials, private data, and unreleased details.
- Do not stage a bug, fake a reaction, or display a specific result that was not provided.
- Use minimal natural cuts. Pauses, imperfect lighting, background noise, and small corrections are acceptable if the audio remains clear.

## Validation

- The spoken script follows the approved Lebanese Arabic version closely.
- The four points remain connected: CS fundamentals, TypeScript, real systems, and product communication.
- CS subjects are framed as concepts to understand and apply, not only requirements to pass.
- TypeScript is presented as a personal web-development choice across frontend and backend work, with Prisma used for the database layer.
- The systems section focuses on communication between parts, data movement, real users, traffic, databases, building, and testing without claiming mastery.
- The communication section includes explaining, presenting, communicating value, and selling a product or solution.
- The teaching-oriented CTA remains at the end of the spoken script and caption.
- First-person student perspective and conversational Lebanese Arabic are maintained.
- No private or unreleased project is named or identifiable.
- No specific bug, feature, result, achievement, or tool experience is invented.
- The Reel uses a real student environment, minimal cuts, and no staged reactions.
- The script is intended to remain under 60 seconds at a natural speaking pace.
