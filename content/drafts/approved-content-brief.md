# Approved Content Brief — Four Areas That Connect CS Study to Building Software

## Working title

Four areas that connect CS study to building software

## Core idea

Starting my second year in Computer Science made me examine how foundations, technical practice, systems thinking, and product communication connect to building better software.

## Trigger

Starting the second year of my CS degree and reflecting on what I want to focus on this year. The original reflection names four priorities: core CS subjects, deeper TypeScript use for web development, understanding systems through real building and users, and learning to present and communicate a product’s value.

## Audience problem

Early developers can treat coursework, learning tools, building projects, and product communication as separate tracks. They may miss how each one informs better technical decisions in the others.

## Key insight

Developer judgment grows through connected foundations, focused practice, understanding how systems behave, and communicating product value. The creator is beginning to explore this connection; this is not a claim of mastery.

## Intended outcome

The audience should leave with a clearer model for how foundations, building, systems understanding, and communication relate. The creator's second-year context should make the idea concrete without turning it into a universal roadmap or a personal update.

## Content pillar

Clear explanations of systems, decisions, and tradeoffs from real software work.

## Target platforms

- **Instagram:** The primary, medium-depth visual narrative for Arabic-speaking CS students and early developers. Use conversational Lebanese Arabic and preserve English technical terms naturally.

## Primary format

Instagram Carousel.

## Creative direction

Treat this as an explanation-led **Project Thinking** carousel about how four areas support better software building. Lead with the connection between them; use the second-year transition as the real trigger and context, not the main subject. Show the reasoning that links each area to building software, then close with the four-part model. Use `Carousels.md` to keep each slide focused; do not turn this into a tutorial, universal advice, or a personal update.

## Supporting context

Source material: the approved brief’s own supporting context, “The Four Things I’m Focusing on This Year.” Preserve its authentic trigger and four priorities:

1. Understand core subjects such as databases, software engineering, and networking.
2. Go deeper with TypeScript for web development across React and Express, with Prisma in the database layer.
3. Understand how systems and their parts behave through building and trying a real system with real users, including frontend/backend communication and handling growth.
4. Learn to present an idea, communicate with people more experienced in the field, and explain and sell the value of a product.

These are stated areas of focus, not completed accomplishments. The source does not identify a specific current project, measured result, or concrete implementation example; do not add one.
